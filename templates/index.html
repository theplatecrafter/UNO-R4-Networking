Step 1: Install Camera App

Install IP Webcam from the Google Play Store on the phone you will mount to the robot.

Step 2: Network Setup

Turn on your Phone's Mobile Hotspot. Connect your laptop to this hotspot.

Provide the network details so we can inject them into the Arduino code.

Step 3: Connect Camera

Open IP Webcam on the phone, tap Start Server. Enter the IP shown at the bottom of the screen.

Failed to connect to camera. Check IP and ensure server is running.

Step 4: Flash the Robot

Your latency-compensating Arduino code is ready. Wi-Fi, server, and motor pin settings will be injected.

Motor Pin Mapping

Enter the Arduino pins connected to each motor driver. EN should be a PWM-capable pin.

Open the Serial Monitor in Arduino IDE. Wait for it to say "WiFi Connected!" and copy the IP Address below.
